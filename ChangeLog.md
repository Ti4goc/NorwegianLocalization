## 1.0.0
* Initial public release of the Norwegian Bokmål localization.
* Added translations for the main interface, options, services, information views and progression systems.
* Added Norwegian names for citizens, roads, bridges, districts and other generated content.
* Added partial translations for tutorials, glossary entries, buildings and assets.
* Added I18NEverywhere integration.
* Some content remains in English and will be translated in future updates.
